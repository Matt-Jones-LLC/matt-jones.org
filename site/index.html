HOW IT’S BUILT

No plugin required.
Just the open web.

Flash needed a plugin. Everything we publish runs on what your browser already ships with, so there is nothing to install and very little to break.

PAGES
HTML, CSS, JavaScriptWritten by hand. No framework and no build step.
GAMES
Canvas and Web AudioPlain JavaScript drawing to the canvas element. Scores and saves stay in your own browser.
TOONS
Native HTML5 videoWritten by Matt, generated with Runway, and played as MP4 by the browser’s own video element.
HOSTING
Static files on a CDNNo servers to run. This site and Flash Is Dead are served from Amazon S3 through CloudFront.
DIAGNOSTICS
Datadog, opt-inPerformance and error monitoring stays off until you turn it on. No session replay.
CHECKS
pytest, Ruff, NodeAutomated tests cover links, content rules and monitoring behavior before a release.

OUR APPROACH

Curiosity starts the work.
Care carries it through.

We believe useful software and memorable play share something important: respect for the person on the other side of the screen. We start with a clear idea, build something tangible, and keep improving it.

Experimentation has its own space. Finished experiences have theirs. That distinction gives us room to try something new while keeping our public releases focused.
